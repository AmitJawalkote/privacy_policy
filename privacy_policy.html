Privacy Policy

App name: Sonai Hostel Management

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Sonai Hostel Management ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how the app is used, we may collect and process:

  • Tenant name and contact information.
  • Guardian name, phone number, and relationship to the tenant.
  • Tenant photographs and identity documents, including Aadhaar images, when provided.
  • Room numbers, bed allocation, address, and joining date.
  • Rent records, payment details, meter readings, and related hostel management information.
  • Account information required for authentication and access.
  • Technical information processed by the services used to operate and secure the app.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Manage tenant registrations, rooms, and bed allocations.
  • Maintain tenant records and identity documents.
  • Track rent payments and hostel-related charges.
  • Manage user accounts and authenticate authorized users.
  • Communicate about hostel administration when necessary.
  • Maintain app security, troubleshoot issues, and improve reliability.

3. Storage and Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Firebase services, which may include Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage, to support account authentication and data storage.

Information may be processed by these service providers according to their applicable privacy terms and security practices.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized hostel administrators and personnel when required for hostel operations. It may also be processed by service providers supporting the app or disclosed when required by applicable law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for hostel administration, legitimate operational needs, and applicable legal requirements. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable obligations and verification requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for hostel administration and authorized users. If information about a minor is entered, it should be provided only by an authorized person where legally permitted. Please contact us if information has been submitted improperly.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us with questions or requests concerning your personal information. We may need to verify your identity and authority before processing a request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
